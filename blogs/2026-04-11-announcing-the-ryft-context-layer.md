Ryft already monitors Iceberg lakehouses for optimization and observability. That means we already collect the signals that matter most for context: schema and structure, query patterns across every engine (Spark, Trino, Snowflake, Athena), write and ingestion behavior, freshness, and statistics. It's the same information a senior analyst would use to understand a table, captured in real time, at infrastructure scale.The Lakehouse Context Layer combines these signals into rich, agent-readable context for every table.
